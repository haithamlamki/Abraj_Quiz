## 5. Decisions and one caveat you should act on

1. **⚠ Competitor data is not independently verified.** The prices and capability
   entries come from your brief; I have no way to confirm third-party vendors'
   current list prices or feature sets, and my own knowledge predates the stated
   28/07/2026 review date. The appendix is hedged ("indicative only", "reflect each
   vendor's published information at that date", "subject to change by those
   vendors"), but **comparative claims about named competitors carry commercial and
   legal exposure if any entry is wrong or out of date.** Please have someone verify
   each row against the vendors' current public pricing pages before this goes to
   PDO, and consider keeping a dated evidence file of those pages.
2. **"400+ concurrent" is qualified**, not stated as a bare capability: the cell
   reads "400+ concurrent per session (internal load testing)", matching how the
   figure is described everywhere else in the suite.
3. **"Oman data residency available"** rather than a flat "data residency in Oman",
   since residency is a deployment-dependent option confirmed at discovery (§17, §19).
   "Local support" is carried in the closing paragraph rather than the table, to keep
   the table to the seven columns you specified.
4. **Executive summaries needed further typographic compaction** to absorb the new
   line and stay on one page: list text 8.8→8.3 pt, table text 8.6→8.3 pt, cell
   padding 2.5→2.2 pt, applied to both languages. No content was cut.
