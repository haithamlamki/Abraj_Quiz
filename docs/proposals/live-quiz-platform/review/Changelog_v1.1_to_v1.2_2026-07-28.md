## Decisions taken where the brief was silent or in tension — please confirm

1. **"Training add-ons" optional row removed.** The all-inclusive principle says no
   separate training charge may appear anywhere, but §3 carried a priced
   "Training add-ons" row that the fill-in map did not cover. Removing it is the only
   way to satisfy the principle. If you would rather keep it as a purchasable extra,
   it is a one-row restore.
2. **Other §3 optional items were not in the map.** No prices were invented: penetration
   testing, 24/7 Gold support, and additional language packs read
   "Scoped and quoted separately per engagement". The source-code licence was set to
   **OMR 1,250** to match Model B rather than left unpriced.
3. **British English retained.** The suite is British English throughout ("licence",
   "customisation"), so the tiers read **"organisation-wide"**, not "organization-wide".
4. **Executive summaries needed typographic compaction.** Adding the Pricing row pushed
   the EN summary to two pages. Body text 9.7→9.2 pt, table text 9→8.6 pt, list text
   9→8.8 pt, line-height 1.4→1.28, cell padding 3.5→2.5 pt — applied to both languages
   so the pair stays visually matched. No content was cut; both are one page.
5. **"An agreed number of sessions" kept** in the Model A description. The brief defined
   the tiers as unlimited hosts and participants but said nothing about session counts,
   and removing the limit would have widened scope.
