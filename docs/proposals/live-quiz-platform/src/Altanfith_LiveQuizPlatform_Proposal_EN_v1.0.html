6. Executive Summary

Altanfith Aldwaliah SPC is pleased to present the Live Quiz Platform to [Client Name] as an enterprise engagement, assessment, and knowledge-evaluation platform — a flexible, customizable alternative to general public quiz tools, purpose-built for organizations that require organizational branding, structured reporting, and enterprise-grade controls around how live sessions and assessments are run.

The platform is not a concept or a prototype. It is already in real operational use at Abraj Energy Services, where it supports HSE meetings and employee engagement activities, with single live sessions that have brought together more than 100 participants at once. This production track record gives [Client Name] confidence that the core platform is stable, functional, and capable of supporting real organizational events at meaningful scale, rather than a solution that exists only on paper.

The platform is bilingual by design, with a fully localized Arabic and English interface including complete right-to-left (RTL) support for Arabic — a capability that matters for organizations operating across Oman and the wider Gulf region, where sessions, dashboards, and reports need to be equally usable in either language.

Content creation is accelerated through AI-assisted generation: administrators can generate a complete quiz from a topic or title, or from an uploaded PDF, Word document, URL, or pasted text, with the AI assigning suggested difficulty levels and answer explanations. Every AI-generated item is presented to an administrator for review and editing before it is used in a live session — the platform is designed to keep a human reviewer in the loop rather than publishing AI output automatically. Additional AI capabilities, such as AI-assisted rewriting, suggestions, and generated visual themes, are available as optional, separately scoped additions, and a further set of AI-driven insights and reporting features sits on our future roadmap.

[Client Name] can choose how the platform is hosted and delivered. Three deployment models are available: a fully managed cloud/SaaS option, a fully on-premises option in which the Express.js backend runs entirely within the client's own infrastructure, and a hybrid option that splits components between the two — the right choice depends on [Client Name]'s data residency, network, and cybersecurity requirements, and is confirmed through a dedicated infrastructure assessment rather than assumed in advance. Commercially, the platform is available under three models — an annual SaaS subscription, a one-time perpetual license, and an enterprise custom development partnership — so that [Client Name] can select the ownership and cost structure that best fits its procurement approach.

Underpinning all of this is the fact that Altanfith Aldwaliah SPC is an Omani company: delivery, support, and communication happen locally, in Arabic and English, without the delays or handover friction that come with an offshore vendor. 11. Platform Overview

The Live Quiz Platform is best understood through the lifecycle of a typical session, from creation through to reporting.

An administrator or quiz creator begins by building a quiz manually, or by using the platform's AI-assisted generation to produce a complete quiz from a topic, an uploaded PDF or Word document, a URL, or pasted text — with every AI-generated question reviewed and editable before use. The quiz is then branded with the organization's own logo, colors, and theme, and can be organized into a reusable question bank tagged by subject and difficulty for future sessions.

When it is time to run a session, the host launches the quiz live. Participants join in seconds using a QR code, a direct link, or a session PIN, without needing to create an account. As the session runs, the platform is server-authoritative for timing and scoring: the server determines when a question opens and closes and calculates results, while participant and host devices render what is happening in real time — including live answer submission, a live leaderboard, and automatic reconnection if a participant's network connection drops mid-session. At the close of the session, the top three participants are shown on a podium, alongside the full participant ranking.

After the session ends, results are not lost. They are captured in the platform's reporting and session history, giving administrators and management access to participation data, performance analysis, and historical comparisons across sessions — described in full in Section 16.
