1. Quotation Summary

Al Tanfith Al Dwaliah SPC is pleased to present this commercial quotation for the Live Quiz Platform to Petroleum Development Oman (PDO) an enterprise engagement, assessment, and knowledge-evaluation platform, offered as a flexible, customisable alternative to general public quiz tools, and already proven in real operational use.

This quotation is a standalone commercial document. It accompanies, and should be read together with, the full technical and commercial proposal (Live Quiz Platform Technical & Commercial Proposal, reference ATD-LQP-2026-003), which sets out the functional scope, technical architecture, deployment options, cybersecurity approach, delivery methodology, support model, and legal terms in full. Where this quotation and the full proposal refer to the same item, the proposal is the definitive description of scope.

The prices in Section 2 for Model A and Model B are firm and all-inclusive: implementation, deployment, branding setup, and training are included, with no separate setup, implementation, or training charges. Model C is scoped and quoted separately per engagement, using the factors listed in Section 4. This quotation is valid for 30 days from 28/07/2026 (until 27/08/2026), as set out in Section 6.

2. Pricing Tables

The Live Quiz Platform is offered under three distinct commercial models, so that PDO can select the ownership, cost, and delivery structure that best fits its procurement approach. The subsections below summarise each model; the comparison table that follows sets them side by side. Prices for Models A and B are firm and all-inclusive implementation, deployment, branding setup, and training are included. Model C is scoped and quoted separately per engagement.

2.1 Model A Annual SaaS Subscription

An annual, subscription-based model in which Altanfith hosts and operates the platform on PDO's behalf. Includes: annual licence; cloud hosting; maintenance; security updates; backup; monitoring; support; standard upgrades; unlimited named administrative accounts and unlimited participant access (see Section 13 of the full proposal), with an agreed number of sessions; implementation, deployment, branding setup, and training; and an AI usage allowance of 500 generations per month, with optional overage pricing. Two tiers are offered single department and organisation-wide each with unlimited hosts and unlimited participants. Deep customisation beyond the standard configuration is handled under Model C.

ItemPrice
Annual Subscription single departmentOMR 300 / year
Annual Subscription organisation-wideOMR 1,200 / year
AI Consumption AllowanceIncluded 500 generations per calendar month; unused generations do not roll over to subsequent months
Overage (beyond agreed usage)OMR 10 per additional 500 generations

2.2 Model B Perpetual Licence / One-Time Purchase

A one-time purchase model suited to organisations that prefer to own the software outright. Includes: one-time software licence; deployment; initial configuration; training; documentation; an optional source-code licence; optional source-code ownership; and optional annual maintenance, recommended at 15% of licence value, payable annually in advance. Implementation, deployment, branding setup, and training are included in the one-time licence fee, with no separate charges. Model B includes a 60-day defect warranty from go-live, covering defects in the delivered functionality; after the warranty period, fixes and updates are provided only under the optional annual maintenance. Major new features are excluded unless separately agreed, and infrastructure costs are excluded unless explicitly listed in this quotation.

An ongoing partnership model for organisations requiring deep customisation and integration work. 5. Commercial Terms

Proposed terms subject to negotiation. The terms below illustrate the structure Altanfith typically proposes; final commercial terms for PDO are agreed in the signed commercial agreement.

  • Currency: Omani Rial (OMR). Amounts are exclusive of applicable taxes unless otherwise stated.
  • Annual subscription (Model A): 100% annually in advance against invoice.
  • Perpetual licence (Model B): 40% upon contract signature, 40% upon User Acceptance Testing (UAT) approval, 20% upon production go-live. This payment schedule is recommended and remains subject to commercial negotiation and mutual agreement.
  • Maintenance (Model B, optional): annual payment in advance.
  • Change requests: quoted and invoiced separately.
  • Delivery mode: all project activities are delivered remotely by default; on-site attendance is quoted separately.
  • Travel outside Muscat: charged separately, where required and agreed in advance.
  • Third-party services: charged at cost, plus an agreed administration fee where applicable.
  • Limitation of liability: Altanfith's aggregate liability shall not exceed the amounts actually paid by the client in the 12 months preceding the claim; neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages subject to the final signed contract and applicable Omani law (see the full proposal, Section 41).
