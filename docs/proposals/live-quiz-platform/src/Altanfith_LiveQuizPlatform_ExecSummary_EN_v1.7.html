Executive Summary Live Quiz Platform

Prepared for: Petroleum Development Oman (PDO)  ·  Technical & Commercial Overview  ·  Version 1.7  ·  28/07/2026

The Live Quiz Platform is an enterprise engagement, assessment, and knowledge-evaluation platform a flexible, customisable alternative to general public quiz tools, purpose-built for corporate and institutional use. It supports HSE meetings, employee training, knowledge assessments, corporate workshops, onboarding, internal compliance checks, and recruitment assessments, delivered through a fully bilingual Arabic and English interface with complete right-to-left support. See competitive analysis in the full proposal, Appendix D.

Proven in production. The platform is in real operational use within Abraj Energy Services for HSE meetings and employee engagement activities evidence that this is a production-proven solution, not a concept or prototype.

Platform AI & Analytics Delivery & Security
  • Live hosting with QR-code, direct-link, and session-PIN joining
  • Real-time answers, live leaderboard, and top-three winners podium
  • Multiple question types, polls, timers, and configurable scoring
  • Question bank with subject, tags, difficulty, and bulk import (Excel / CSV / Word)
  • Custom branding, themes, and experience tailored to the institution
  • Generate a quiz from a topic, PDF, document, URL, or pasted text
  • AI-assigned difficulty levels and answer explanations
  • All AI content presented for administrator review before use
  • Result summaries, participant rankings, and answer analysis
  • Export to PDF, Excel, and CSV with Arabic and English headers
  • SaaS, on-premises, or hybrid deployment (React front end, Express.js and PostgreSQL back end)
  • Role-based access, secure data isolation, and administrative audit logs
  • Secure password hashing, TLS, and API rate limiting, developed with awareness of the OWASP Top 10
  • Structured Bronze / Silver / Gold support with an agreed SLA
  • Phased delivery with training and knowledge transfer
